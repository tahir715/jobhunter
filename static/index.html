How Job Hunter works

Three simple steps, so you spend less time hunting and more time applying.

1

We check official sites

Ministry, department and university websites are checked for new job advertisements.

2

Deadlines are read for you

Closing dates are picked out of each advertisement, so you can see how many days are left.

3

One clean list

Tenders, results and expired ads are filtered out. Always confirm details on the official advertisement.

Job Hunter · An independent tool. Data is collected from official ministry and department websites.
